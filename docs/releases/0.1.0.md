# FF8Recomp 0.1.0

The first build: FINAL FANTASY VIII recompiled to native ARM64 Android, built on your device from
your own copy, the 2013 Steam release or the Remastered. The APK contains no game code or data.

## What works
- **Your game, either edition.** Connect to Steam (a QR code scanned with the Steam app; no
  password is typed into FF8Recomp) and choose which FINAL FANTASY VIII to play, or pick the
  game's folder on your device. The launcher builds the game for your device once (about 15
  minutes on an AYN Thor) and you can switch editions later.
- **The Remastered** through FFNx's Remastered mode: its HD models, portraits and menus, its own
  recording of the music and its movies, in English, French, German, Spanish or Italian.
- **The 2013 release**, with its music played by FF8Recomp's own DirectMusic player and the
  game's own instruments (`FF8.dls`).
- The game: title screen, movies, fields, the world map, menus, battles and GF summons (tested
  on an AYN Thor). Battles at 30 FPS (the game shipped them at 15; the original timing and an
  experimental 60 FPS are in Settings).
- **Mods**: Junction VIII's catalogs (the Tsunamods catalog by default) and `.iro`/`.iroj`
  files, with their options. `.hext` code patches work; mods with Windows DLLs are skipped.
- **The second screen** on dual-screen devices: the party with portraits, HP and ATB, junctions,
  magic, GFs, items, Triple Triad cards, enemies in battle, the place you are in, and buttons
  for the menu, save states, 2x speed and random battles off.
- Your PC saves (`slot1_save01.ff8`…) under `Android/data/com.ff8recomp/files/game/save/user_0/`.
- Save states (Select+L1 / Select+R1), 16:9, Vulkan or OpenGL ES, controller, keyboard and touch
  controls, Report a problem.

## Not yet

## Install
Download `FF8Recomp-0.1.0.apk` below and install it, then follow **Game files** in the launcher.
See the [README](../../README.md) for details.

This project was built with AI (see the README's "Made with AI"). Please report problems with
**Report a problem** in the launcher.
