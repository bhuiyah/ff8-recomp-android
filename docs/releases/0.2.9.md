# FF8Recomp 0.2.9

Photos of the change are at the end of these notes.

**New: EXP from battles** (#20). **Settings > Battles > EXP from battles** sets how much EXP the party gets
from a battle: 0×, 1× (the game's), 2×, 5× or 10×.
- **0×** is for low-level play. The party gets no EXP, so they stay at their level, and so do the enemies, whose
  levels follow the party's. The results screen shows 0 and the game's own "Didn't receive EXP".
- GFs still get their usual EXP, and AP is unchanged.
- The results screen shows the EXP that is actually given, killing-blow bonus included.

It applies from the next time you press Play.

Tested on an AYN Thor in battles on the world map, with the Remastered: 0× (no EXP; the GFs still got theirs),
2×, 5× and 10× (EXP added to the characters; with 10× all three went up a level); and with the 2013 release: 0×.

### Updating
Install `FF8Recomp-0.2.9.apk` over 0.2.8 (the launcher also offers it), then press **Update the game** when the
launcher asks (a few seconds) and Play. Your saves, mods and settings stay. Save states made with an earlier
version may not load, so make a normal save in the game before you update.
