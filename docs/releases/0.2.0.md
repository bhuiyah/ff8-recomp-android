# FF8Recomp 0.2.0

**Battles no longer run too fast.** The default frame rate, "30 FPS battles", played battles at twice
their speed, and "60 FPS" at four times: FF8 counts its frames, and more of them means everything
happens sooner. The default is now the game's own speed (battles at 15 FPS, the rest at 30), and
anyone on one of those two is moved to it once. They are still there, as Fast battles and Fast
everything.

**Frame generation** (#8): up to 60 FPS at the game's own speed, with frames made between the game's
by Lossless Scaling, if you own it on Steam. Settings > Display > Frame generation downloads
Lossless.dll (7 MB) from your Steam account; FF8Recomp does not include it. It needs the Vulkan
renderer and a Vulkan 1.3 driver. It adds a delay (about 45 ms when walking, more in battles) and
uses more battery, and a camera cut blends for a few frames. Off by default.

**Both editions side by side.** The 2013 release and the Remastered can be installed together and
share your saves: switch under Game files, without downloading or building again. Connecting to Steam
offers **Download both**.

**Fixed: battles on the Remastered could crash** when an enemy used a certain attack (FFNx makes room
for the Remastered's more detailed models in five of the game's six copies of that effect; now in all).

**Mods with `.dds` textures** (#9): HD battle, world map and character texture mods that shipped them were
skipped; they are read now.

**Mods on the Remastered** (#4, #9, #10): mods can now replace files inside the Remastered's archives, which
Demaster mods do (a `DEMASTER_EXP` folder, or `data/` and `textures/`). Before, those files were ignored.
Field and world files made for the other edition are left out, as they would turn every area black.

**Everything stretched to fill** (#9): a new picture option in Settings > Display that stretches fields and
movies too, with no side bars.

**Controller** (#7): Settings now lists the in-game shortcuts (speed, random battles, skip a movie, save
states) and lets you swap what the buttons do. The 16:9 option says where it applies.

**Phones with 2 GB of memory** (#5) compile one file at a time, so a build does not run out of memory. It
still takes many hours on them.

Also: a build no longer fails now and then on one file, and updates take less time to translate.

### Updating
Install `FF8Recomp-0.2.0.apk` over 0.1.x (the launcher also offers it) and press **Build for this
device**. From 0.1.2 this takes a few minutes; from an older version it is one more full build.
Save states made with earlier versions may not load; the game's own saves are not affected.
